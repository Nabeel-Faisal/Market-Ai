These faces are self-hosted rather than loaded from a font CDN, so no visitor
IP addresses are handed to a third party.

---

Files here are the `latin` (and for JetBrains Mono, `latin-ext`) subsets in
woff2 only. If the site ever needs Cyrillic or Greek, pull the matching subsets
rather than swapping in a full unsubsetted file.
